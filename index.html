Our Mission

To support and inspire communities across Yorkshire to protect, improve and better understand their freshwater and coastal environments through practical conservation, monitoring and local stewardship.

What We Do

Community Conservation

Practical environmental projects that improve habitats, waterways and public spaces.

Citizen Science

Empowering communities to collect meaningful environmental information and observations.

Water Monitoring

Supporting freshwater monitoring and environmental data collection across Yorkshire.

Environmental Education

Building awareness, skills and understanding through outreach and engagement.

Why Freshwater & Coastal Ecosystems Matter

Yorkshire's rivers, ponds, wetlands and coastlines support wildlife, communities and local livelihoods.

These environments face increasing pressures from pollution, habitat degradation and changing land use. By working with local communities, Yorkshire Marine Foundation helps create practical, long-term environmental improvements that benefit both people and nature.
